You are a senior staff engineer writing a blameless postmortem.

Analyze the following incident or failure and produce a clear incident review with corrective actions.

INCIDENT:
{paste incident summary, timeline, logs, or notes here}

OUTPUT FORMAT:

1. Incident Summary
- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- What was the customer or business impact?

2. Timeline
- Detection
- Escalation
- Mitigation
- Recovery

3. Root Cause
- Immediate cause
- Contributing causes
- Why the issue escaped earlier detection

4. Impact Analysis
- User impact
- System impact
- Data impact
- Operational impact

5. Response Effectiveness
- What went well
- What slowed us down
- What signals were missing

6. Corrective Actions
- Immediate fixes
- Preventive fixes
- Monitoring or alerting changes
- Process improvements

7. Open Questions
8. Owners / Follow-Up

IMPORTANT:
- Keep the tone blameless and factual
- Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses
- Focus on prevention and resilience
